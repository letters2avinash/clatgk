An India-UAE joint military exercise, Mountain Guardian 2026, began on 5 October 2026 at the Foreign Training Node in Chaubatia, Uttarakhand. The exercise will run until 18 October and focuses on mountain warfare. It is aimed at enhancing interoperability, mutual understanding and cooperation between the two armies. Each side has deployed 46 personnel, bringing the total strength of the exercise to 92. The Indian contingent is drawn from a Bhairav Battalion, while the UAE contingent comes from a Mountain Infantry Battalion. Women officers and combatants from both armies are taking part in specialist roles. Planned training includes joint planning, tactical drills, firing practice, surveillance grids, drone operations, physical endurance training, rock climbing, obstacle training and integrated field exercises.

On 8 October 2026, the Indian Air Force will observe Air Force Day, marking the 94th anniversary of the force. The force was established under the Indian Air Force Act, 1932, which took effect on 8 October 1932. The main parade is scheduled at Hindon Air Force Station in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. The day is marked each year with a parade, a flypast and air displays. This year's theme is "Indian Air Force - Infallible, Impervious and Precise."

Earlier, on 21 September 2026, the Ministry of Defence signed a contract worth about Rs 586 crore with Accurate Industrial Controls Private Limited in New Delhi. The contract covers auxiliary power units for the Army's T-72 and T-90 tanks and was placed under the Make-II category, which is meant for industry-led research and development. Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh was present at the signing. The units supply secondary electricity to the tank's fire control system, firing circuit and gun stabilisation system. The ministry said the project is a step toward indigenising critical tank subsystems.
