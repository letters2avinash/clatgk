International Energy Market by managing Supply Levels. Much like a financial institution, OPEC implements  
Production Quotas to prevent Extreme Fluctuations in Cost of Crude Oil. These strategic limits protect against  
Market Oversupply, ensuring that Prices Remain Sustainable for Both Consumers and Exporting Nations. By  
adjusting output in response to Global Demand, OPEC safeguards Economic Stability of its members who  
depend on Petroleum Revenue. Ultimately, this coordinated approach serves to Minimise Volatility and  
Maintain a Reliable Balance within Global Economy.
