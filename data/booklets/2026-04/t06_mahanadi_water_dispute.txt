Where does the Mahanadi River originate?  
(a) Amarkantak Hills, Madhya Pradesh (b) Sihawa Hill, Chhattisgarh  
(c) Satpura Range, Maharashtra (d) Vindhya Hills, Uttar Pradesh  
  
Into which water body does the Mahanadi River ultimately empty?  
(a) Arabian Sea (b) Gulf of Mannar (c) Bay of Bengal (d) Gulf of Kutch  
  
What is the significance of the Hirakud Dam built on the Mahanadi River?  
(a) It is the tallest concrete dam in India  
  
(b) It is the longest earthen dam in the world  
  
(c) It is the first hydroelectric dam in India  
  
(d) It is the largest reservoir in Asia by surface area  
  
Around which year did the Mahanadi water dispute between Odisha and Chhattisgarh significantly escalate?  
(a) 2008 (b) 2012 (c) 2016 (d) 2019  
  
Under which Act was the Mahanadi Water Disputes Tribunal constituted?  
(a) River Boards Act, 1956 (b) Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956  
(c) Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 (d) National Water Policy Act, 2002  
  
Which Article of the Indian Constitution empowers Parliament to adjudicate disputes relating to inter-state rivers?  
(a) Article 245 (b) Article 256 (c) Article 262 (d) Article 280  
  
What does the River Boards Act 1956 empower the Government of India to do?  
  
(a) Unilaterally allocate water shares to states  
  
(b) Establish Boards for Inter-State Rivers and River Valleys in consultation with State Governments  
(c) Construct dams on inter-state rivers without state consent  
  
(d) Impose financial penalties on states violating water-sharing agreements  
  
How many River Boards have been created under the River Boards Act, 1956 as of now?  
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c)7 (d) None  
  
What is the Supreme Court's role with respect to the award or formula given by an Inter-State Water Disputes  
  
Tribunal?  
  
(a) The Supreme Court can fully override the Tribunal's award  
  
(b) The Supreme Court shall not question the award or formula given by the Tribunal, but can question the working of  
the Tribunal  
  
(c) The Supreme Court must ratify all Tribunal awards before they become legally binding  
  
(d) The Supreme Court has no jurisdiction whatsoever over Inter-State Water Tribunals
