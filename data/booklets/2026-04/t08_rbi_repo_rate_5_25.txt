Inflation Targeting and India  
e Inflation Targeting: Monetary Policy Framework where Central Bank aims to maintain Rate of Inflation within a  
Targeted (Pre—Defined) Range.  
e India’s Inflation Targeting Framework: India adopted Flexible Inflation Targeting in 2016, with an Inflation Target of  
4% (with a deviation of +/- 2%).  
o Urjit Patel Committee (2014) Formalizes Inflation Targeting in India, Setting 4% Inflation Target with 2% Band, a  
target first proposed by Chakravarty Committee (1982 —85)  
o Inflation Target is set by Government in consultation with RBI every 5 Years, under amended RBI Act, 1934.
