PRACTICE QUESTIONS  
  
What milestone does the Census 2027 represent in India's census history?  
(a) First census conducted after Independence (b) First Fully Digital Enumeration Exercise  
(c) First census to include overseas Indian citizens (d) First census conducted by an independent statutory body  
  
Who conducted India's first Synchronous Census in 1881, and under whose viceroyalty was it held?  
(a) Lord Curzon; conducted by Henry Cotton (b) Lord Rippon; conducted by W.C. Plowden  
(c) Lord Mayo; conducted by B.N. Rau (d) Lord Lytton; conducted by Alfred Lyall  
  
When was India's first Non-Synchronous National Census conducted, and under whose viceroyalty?  
(a) 1861, Lord Canning (b) 1871, Lord Mayo (c) 1881, Lord Rippon (d) 1891, Lord Lansdowne  
  
Which was the last caste-based census conducted in India?  
(a) 1921 Census (b) 1931 Census (c) 1941 Census (d) 1951 Census  
  
How many questions does the Census 2027 questionnaire contain?  
(a) 24 (b) 28 (c) 33 (d) 40  
  
In how many languages is the Self-Enumeration Facility for Census 2027 available?  
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 16 (d) 22  
  
Under which Article and Entry of the Constitution does the Union have the power to conduct Census?  
(a) Article 245 and Entry 65 (b) Article 246 and Entry 69  
(c) Article 248 and Entry 72 (d) Article 250 and Entry 75  
  
What did the 84th Constitutional Amendment Act of 2002 do regarding Lok Sabha seat allocation?  
(a) Increased the number of Lok Sabha seats from 525 to 543  
  
(b) Froze Lok Sabha seat allocation using 1971 Census data until the first census conducted post-2026  
(c) Introduced reservation of seats for women in Lok Sabha  
  
(d) Transferred delimitation powers from Parliament to the Supreme Court  
  
Which Act provides the legal mandate for the Census, making participation mandatory?  
(a) Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969 (b) Census Act, 1948  
(c) National Population Register Act, 2003 (d) Statistics Collection Act, 2008  
  
Who manages the Census 2027 as the Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India?  
(a) Vivek Joshi (b) Sailesh  
(c) Mritunjay Kumar Narayan (d) Ashutosh Shukla  
  
How will caste data from Census 2027 aid electoral planning?  
  
(a) It will determine the number of parliamentary seats for each state  
  
(b) It will aid Delimitation and enable implementation of Women's One-Third Seat Reservation in Parliament and  
Assemblies  
  
(c) It will determine the composition of the Rajya Sabha  
  
(d) It will be used to decide reservation percentages in government jobs

What is the National Population Register (NPR), and who falls within its scope?  
  
(a) A register of only Indian citizens; foreigners excluded  
  
(b) A database of every resident including foreigners who has lived at an address for the last 6 months and intends to  
stay for the next 6 months  
  
(c) A register of overseas Indians and NRIs  
  
(d) A register maintained by the Home Ministry to track illegal immigrants  
  
What is the key difference between NPR and NRC?  
  
(a) NPR covers only citizens; NRC covers all residents including foreigners  
  
(b) NPR is a database of all residents (including foreigners) for welfare purposes; NRC identifies only Citizens of  
India with valid identity documents  
  
(c) NPR is managed by the Election Commission; NRC is managed by the Home Ministry  
  
(d) NPR applies only to urban areas; NRC applies nationwide  
  
What is the scope of the Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA) regarding who is included?  
  
(a) All refugees from neighbouring countries regardless of religion  
  
(b) Minority refugees (Hindu, Christian, Sikh, Parsi, Jain, Buddhist) from Pakistan, Afghanistan and Bangladesh who  
came before 31 December 2014  
  
(c) All economic migrants from South Asian countries  
  
(d) Only Hindu refugees from Pakistan and Bangladesh  
  
Currently, which is the only state that updates the National Register of Citizens (NRC)?  
(a) West Bengal (b) Tripura (c) Manipur (d) Assam.
