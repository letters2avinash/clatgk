e Positive Impacts  
o Boost to Exports: Indian Goods become Cheaper globally, improving Competitiveness.  
o Higher Value of Remittances: NRIs send money ata More Favourable Exchange Rate.  
  
o Pushto Domestic Manufacturing: Expensive Imports encourage Local Production.  
e Negative Impacts  
o Costlier External Debt: Companies with Dollar — denominated Loans face Higher Repayment and Interest Costs.  
o Widening Current Account Deficit & Trade Deficit: Costlier Imports Worsen External Balances.  
o Higher Subsidy Burden: Fertilizer imports become Costlier \> Larger Subsidy Bill.  
o Imported Inflation: Crude, Fertilisers, Electronics become Costlier.  
o Capital Flight Risk: Weak Rupee Reduces Investor Confidence.  
  
o Lower Consumer Purchasing Power: Costlier Essential Imports.
