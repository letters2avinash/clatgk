Which type of polygamy involves one woman having multiple husbands?  
(a) Polygyny (b) Polyandry (c) Bigamy (d) Monogamy

(a) Assam (b) Meghalaya (c) West Bengal (d) Tripura  
  
Under the Assam Prohibition of Polygamy Bill 2025, who is primarily targeted by the law?  
(a) Residents of Assam (b) All Indians  
(c) Only government employees (d) Only men  
  
Which communities are excluded from the Assam Prohibition of Polygamy Bill?  
(a) Scheduled Tribes under Article 342 and 6th Schedule Areas  
  
(b) Muslims  
  
(c) Hindus  
  
(d) Christians  
  
What is the maximum imprisonment under this Bill for entering a second marriage while the first is still valid?  
(a) 5 years (b) 7 years (c) 10 years (d) 14 years

(a) Invalidates previous polygamous marriages  
  
(b) Validates pre-law marriages compliant with personal or customary laws  
(c) Exempts all elderly citizens  
  
(d) Protects patriarchal systems  
  
What fund does the Bill introduce for women affected by polygamy?  
(a) Legal Aid Fund (b) State Victim Relief Fund  
(c) Rehabilitation Fund (d) Housing Fund  
  
Who can be held accountable for facilitating polygamous marriages under this Bill?  
(a) Only the individual entering marriage (b) Village Heads, Qazis, Parents, and Legal Guardians  
(c) Only priests (d) Only government officials  
  
Are individuals convicted under this Bill allowed to participate in elections?  
(a) Yes (b) No, they are barred from electoral participation  
(c) Only in local elections (d) After serving sentence  
  
Does the Bill apply to marriages conducted outside Assam?  
  
(a) No  
  
(b) Yes, if individuals engaged in polygamous marriages outside the state  
(c) Only if registered in Assam  
  
(d) Only government employees  
  
Which legal framework does the Bill aim to strengthen?  
(a) Criminal law (b) Family law ensuring monogamous marriages  
(c) Property law (d) Constitutional law
