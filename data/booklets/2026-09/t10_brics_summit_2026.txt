Financial, Economic and Trade Cooperation  
  
BRICS Payment Task Force: Initiative seeks to facilitate Trade Settlements through Local Currencies and Reduce Exposure to  
  
Exchange —Rate Volatility and External Financial Disruptions.  
  
BRICS Risk Lab (GIFT City, Gujarat): Aims to strengthen Reinsurance Capabilities and address Geopolitical Risks associated  
with Large Infrastructure Projects in Global South.  
  
Jaipur Consensus on MSME Financing: Mechanism is intended to help MSMEs convert Unpaid Invoices into Working Capital  
  
and enhance their participation in International Trade.  
  
e New Development Bank: BRICS—NDB Knowledge Portal was launched.  
  
Startup Financing: Proposed a BRICS Startup Innovation Fund to support Early — Stage Ventures.  
Taxation and Customs Cooperation  
e Launched BRICS Tax Cross — Learning Lab and Authorised Economic Operator Action Plan 2026.  
e Envisaged Annual Joint Customs Enforcement Operations to counter Smuggling Networks, address Illicit Financial Flows,  
facilitate Legitimate Trade.  
Science, Technology and Digital Infrastructure  
e Space Cooperation: Terms were drafted for a proposed BRICS Space Council.  
o Cooperation under BRICS Remote Sensing Satellite Constellation was expanded.  
\= Collective Satellite Data would support Disaster Risk Reduction, Climate Monitoring and Wider Access to Space —based  
Technologies.  
e — Digital Public Infrastructure: Approved creation of a BRICS DPI Repository.  
o Pilot Projects were initiated for Sharing scalable and Open-Source Digital Public Goods, including Digital identity Systems  
and Digital Payment Systems.  
e Global Research Advanced Infrastructure Network: An Action Plan was adopted for Global Research Advanced Infrastructure  
Network (GRAIN).  
o Aim: To pool Financial and Intellectual Resources for High— Cost Megascience Projects.  
e Smart Grids and Startups: A Digital Centre of Excellence for Smart Grids was established to support AI — enabled integration of  
Renewable Energy.  
e — BRICS Incubator Network was launched to promote Entrepreneurship and Innovation —led Growth.  
Agriculture and Food Security  
e —BRICS Grain Exchange: BRICS aims to launch BRICS Grain Exchange by 2027.  
o Originally proposed in 2023 and championed by Russia.  
o Aim: To Strengthen Food Security, Reduce Grain — Price Volatility and Promote Intra— BRICS Agricultural Trade.  
e BRICS AGRIN: BRICS AGRIN (Agro-Inputs, Genetic Resources, and Information Network) provides a Collaborative and  
Farmer—centric Framework.  
o Focuses on cooperation relating to Seeds, Germplasm, Agricultural Inputs.  
e Global Forum on Farmers' Rights in Seed Systems: To address Farmers' Rights, Traditional Agricultural Knowledge and  
Common Challenges in Seed Systems.  
e Centres of Excellence on Agroecology and Regenerative Agriculture: Established to promote Sustainable Farming Practices,  
Climate Resilience and Agricultural Productivity.  
  
e BRICS Network on Digital Agriculture: To expand Technology — based Solutions across Agriculture, Agroforestry, Fisheries,  
Aquaculture.
