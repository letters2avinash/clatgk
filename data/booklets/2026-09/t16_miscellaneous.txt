Rules will come into force 180 days after their publication in Official Gazette.  
  
Rules have been notified under Section 52 of Legal Metrology Act, 2009.  
They provide a Statutory Framework for ensuring use of a Uniform and Precise National Time Standard across sectors.  
Framework promotes use of NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) & Indigenous Timing Infrastructure.  
  
Rules form part of broader 'One Nation, One Time' initiative, aimed at establishing a unified and highly accurate national  
  
time standard.
