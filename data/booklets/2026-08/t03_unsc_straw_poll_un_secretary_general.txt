What are the three possible positions a UNSC member can indicate in a straw poll?  
(a) Yes, No, Abstain (b) Encourage, Discourage, No Opinion  
(c) Support, Reject, Veto (d) Approve, Deny, Pending  
  
What is distinctive about the P5's participation in subsequent straw poll rounds?  
(a) They vote publicly (b) They use distinctively coloured ballots  
  
(c) They are excluded from voting (d) They vote via written statements only  
  
What does a "Discourage" indication from a Permanent Member effectively signal?  
(a) Strong support for the candidate (b) That the candidate would face a veto in a formal vote  
  
(c) A procedural error (d) A request for more nominees  
  
Until when do straw poll rounds continue?  
(a) A fixed number of 3 rounds (b) Until a candidate demonstrates sufficient support  
  
(c) Until the General Assembly intervenes (d) Until the outgoing Secretary-General approves  
  
Does the UN Charter prescribe a fixed term limit for the Secretary-General?  
(a) Yes, exactly 5 years (b) Yes, exactly 10 years  
  
(c) No, it does not prescribe a fixed term (d) No, it prescribes a lifetime term  
  
What is the established UN practice regarding the Secretary-General's term length?  
(a) 3-year term (b) 5-year term (c) 7-year term (d) 10-year term  
  
How is the Secretary-General's term conventionally treated regarding renewal?  
(a) Not renewable at all  
  
(b) Renewable once, based on practice rather than an explicit Charter provision  
(c) Renewable indefinitely per Charter mandate  
  
(d) Renewable only with UNSC veto power  
  
How does the General Assembly typically make appointments, despite having the power to decide by majority vote?  
(a) Through secret ballot only (b) Through consensus or acclamation  
  
(c) Through a randomized lottery (d) Through P5 veto approval  
  
What informal principle has traditionally guided the selection of the Secretary-General?  
(a) Alphabetical rotation of countries (b) Regional rotation to promote geographical representation  
(c) Seniority within the UN Secretariat (d) Rotation based on GDP ranking  
  
Is regional rotation a legally binding provision of the UN Charter?  
(a) Yes, explicitly stated in Article 97  
  
(b) Yes, it is enforced by the ICJ  
  
(c) No, it is a political convention, not a legally binding provision  
(d) No, it was abolished in 1994
