Proclaimed: 1823

o. Expanded through Roosevelt Corollary (1904): Justified U.S. Intervention to Maintain Stability in  
Western Hemisphere.  
  
o. In Cold War Era, it was used to Counter Soviet Influence in Americas.  
  
Objective: Establish U.S. Dominance and Security in Western Hemisphere & Prevent European Political  
and Military Influence in Americas.
