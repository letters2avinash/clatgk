History of Republic Day  
  
Constitution of India repealed Indian Independence Act  
1947 and Government of India Act 1935 when it became  
effective on 26th Jan 1950.  
  
India ceased to be a Dominion of British Crown and  
became a Sovereign, Democratic Republic with a  
Constitution.  
  
e Lahore Session of INC in Dec 1929 passed historic "Poorna Swaraj" resolution, calling for Total Self - Rule /  
  
Sovereignty and Complete Independence from British Rule.

Declaration of Independence was officially promulgated on 26" Jan 1930, and Congress urged Indians to celebrate  
“Independence" on that day.  
  
From 1930 to 1947, "26" Jan" was celebrated as "Independence Day" or "Poorna Swaraj Day."  
  
¢ India gained independence on 15" Aug 1947, leading to a re-evaluation of significance of Republic Day.

Extra Shots  
On Republic Day, every year, President of India, who is Head of State, "Unfurls" Tricolour.  
o While on Independence Day (Aug 15), Prime Minister, who heads Union Government, "Hoists" National  
Flag.  
On 26" Jan, Flag is Folded or Rolled, and attached to Top of a Pole.  
o It is then Unveiled (Unfurled) by President, who does so without pulling it up.  
  
\= "Unfurling" is a Symbolic Gesture to Renew Commitment to Principles laid down in the Constitution,  
highlighting India's shift from being a British Colony to becoming a Sovereign, Democratic Republic.  
  
On 15" Aug; Flag, positioned at Bottom of Pole, is Raised (Hoisted) by Prime Minister, from Bottom to Top.  
o Hoisting symbolises Rise of a New Nation; Patriotism and Freedom from Colonial Rule.
