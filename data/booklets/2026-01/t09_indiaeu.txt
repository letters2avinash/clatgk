Long Negotiation Cycle: Agreement, officially known as India — EU Broad = based Trade and Investment  
Agreement (BTIA), brings to a close nearly Two Decades of Intermittent Negotiations, which began in 2007,  
suspended in 2013 & later revived in 2022.  
  
Trade Diversification: It aligns with India's Broader Effort to Diversify Trade Partnerships amid Global  
Supply Chain Disruptions and supports emerging “China — plus One” Strategy.

Key Outcomes of FTA  
  
1\. TariffElimination and Market Access  
¢ Overall Duty Reduction: Duties on over 90% of Bilateral Trade have been Removed.  
  
e EU Commitments: EU will Eliminate Customs Duties on 99.5% of Indian Exports, injecting an estimated $33 Billion  
boost into Labour — Intensive Sectors such as Textiles & Apparel, Leather & Footwear, and Gems & Jewellery, now  
entering EU at 0% Duty.  
  
e India's Commitments: India will Phase Out Tariffs on over 90% of EU Goods, including High Tariffs on Machinery (up

annually.

e¢ Luxury Automobiles: Import Tariffs on Cars priced above €15,000 will Decline from 110% to 10% over 5 Years, witha

e Wines and Spirits: Tariffs on Premium Wines will Fall from 150% to 20—30%, while Spirits will reduce to 40%.  
  
3\. Services Sector and Professional Mobility  
  
e Talent Mobility: Agreement provides Uncapped Mobility for Indian Students, Streamlined Visas for Independent  
Professionals and Intra— Corporate Transferees.

o Legal Basis: Part of EU's Fit for 55 Climate Package aligned with its Net— Zero Targets.

o Climate Integrity: Avoid undermining EU's Domestic Decarbonisation Efforts.
