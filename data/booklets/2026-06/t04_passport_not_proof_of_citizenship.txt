Citizenship  
  
Citizenship is the legal recognition of an individual as a member of a state, granting rights and  
privileges, and requiring allegiance. In India, it defines who holds these rights and follows  
citizenship laws.  
  
Constitutional Provisions Related to Citizenship  
  
Articles 5 to 11 of the Indian Constitution deal with citizenship provisions, specifically outlining  
who became citizens at the commencement of the Constitution (January 26, 1950).

not conclusive proof of citizenship?  
  
(a) Constitution Day (November 26) (b) National Voters' Day (January 25)  
  
(c) Passport Seva Divas (June 24) (d) Independence Day (August 15)

interest?  
(a) Section 10 (b) Section 15 (c) Section 20 (d) Section 25

(a) Kesavananda Bharati vs State of Kerala (b) Maneka Gandhi vs Union of India  
(c) State of Andhra Pradesh vs Abdul Khader (d) Sarbananda Sonowal vs Union of India

According to Association for Democratic Reforms vs ECI (2026), under which Article was the constitutional validity  
of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) upheld?  
(a) Article 326 (b) Article 300A (c) Article 324 (d) Article 368
