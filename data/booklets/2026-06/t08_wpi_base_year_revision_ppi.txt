Why in News?  
Government of India has approved revision of Wholesale Price Index (WPI) Base Year from 2011 — 12 to 2022-23.  
Alongside revised WPI series, Government will introduce new Producer Price Indices (PPIs) to provide a more  
  
comprehensive assessment of Producer -—level Inflation and Cost Dynamics in economy.  
  
Wholesale Price Index  
  
e Measures Average Change in Prices of Goods traded at Wholesale or Producer Level before they reach Final Consumers.  
e Serves as an Important Indicator of Inflationary Trends within Production and Distribution Chain.  
  
e Compiled and Released By: Office of Economic Adviser (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade).  
  
Particulars WPI (2011-12) | WPI (2022-23)  
Number of Items 697 957  
  
\[Major Reforms in Revised WPI Series\!  
  
e — Inclusion of Renewable Energy: New Components Added: Solar Power, Wind Energy, Nuclear Electricity.  
  
e Reclassification of Energy Commodities: Crude Petroleum and Natural Gas have been shifted from Primary Articles  
category to Fuel and Power category.  
  
o Purpose: Improves Sectoral Classification and Better Reflects  
  
Economic Role of Energy Commodities. Advantages  
  
Better reflects importance of Commodities }  
  
e Improved Weighting Methodology: Weights are now derived ‘froma Producer's Perspective.  
  
directly from Gross Value of Output. H . . i  
ie Provides a More Accurate Representation :  
  
o Earlier Method: Weights were based on Net Traded Value of Domestic Production Patterns.  
  
(Gross Value of Output + Imports — Exports). Enhances relevance of WPI as a Producer —  
  
focused Inflation Indicator.  
  
e Enhanced Treatment of Missing Data  
  
o NewApproach: Targeted Mean Imputation  
  
o Previous Approach: Carry — Forward Method (Missing Prices were replaced by Last Available Price).  
  
Introduction of Producer Price Indices  
  
e For first time, India will release dedicated Producer Price Indices with 2022 — 23 as Base Year.  
e Provide a broader assessment of Price Changes experienced by Producers.  
  
e Output Producer Price Index: Measures Changes in Prices received by Producers for Goods and Services they sell.  
  
o Captures prices at Factory Gate or Producer level before Taxes, Trade Margins and Transportation Costs.

The Government of India has revised the WPI Base Year from 2011—12 to which new base year?  
(a) 2017-18 (b) 2019-20 (c) 2020-21 (d) 2022-23  
  
What does WPI stand for?  
(a) Weighted Price Indicator (b) Wholesale Price Index  
(c) Wholesale Production Index (d) Weighted Production Indicator

(a) Changes in retail prices paid by final consumers  
  
(b) Changes in export and import prices
