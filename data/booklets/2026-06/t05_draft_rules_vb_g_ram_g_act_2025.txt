Why in News?  
  
Union Government has released Draft Rules for Viksit Bharat— Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (VB—G RAM  
G) Act 2025, which is set to replace Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act from July 2026.  
  
VB-G RAM G  
VB-—GRAM Gstands for Viksit Bharat— Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission—Gramin.  
  
Rural Employment and Livelihood Programme designed to combine Wage Employment with Creation of Durable Rural  
Assets and Infrastructure.  
  
Objective: Enhance Livelihood Security in Rural Areas, Generate Productive Employment, Strengthen Rural Infrastructure,  
Improve Climate Resilience and Local Planning Outcomes.  
  
Salient Features of VB — G RAM G Act 2025  
  
Enhanced Employment Guarantee: Guaranteed Wage Employment increased from 100 Days to 125 Days  
annually per Rural Household.  
o = Mandatory Payment within 15 Days of Work Completion. :  
e = Agricultural Season Pause: States may declare a Cumulative 60 — day No — Work Period during Peak Sowing and  
Harvesting Seasons.  
o = Objective: Prevent Labour Shortages in Agriculture and Balance Farm Labour Demand with Employment  
  
Guarantee Objectives.  
Rural Infrastructure-Centric Development: Viksit Bharat National Rural Infrastructure Stack Programme links  
Employment Generation with creation of Water Conservation Assets, Rural Connectivity Infrastructure, i  
Livelihood—supporting Facilities, Climate —resilient Community Assets.  
Decentralised and Technology — based Planning: Mandatory Preparation of Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans.  
o Technology Integration: Use of Yuktdhara Portal and Integration with PM Gati Shakti.  
  
Key Highlights of Draft Rules  
  
Shift to a Normative Allocation Model: Draft Rules move away from MGNREGA's Open — ended, Demand — driven  
Financing Model.  
  
o Central Allocations will be determined through a Normative Allocation Formula based on devolution criteria  
  
recommended by 16" Finance Commission.  
o Significance: Promotes Predictability in Resource Allocation.  
\= Aligns Rural Employment Funding with broader Fiscal Federalism Principles.  
Greater Allocation to Economically Weaker States: Promote Equitable Distribution of Resources across States.  
  
o GSDP Distance (42.5% Weightage): Measures Gap between a State's Per Capita Income and that of Wealthiest States.
