Appointment of Prime Minister  
  
e Prime Minister is appointed by "President".  
o But President does Not have Freedom in Selection of Prime Minister.  
e Normally, President has to invite "Leader of Majority Party" to form the Government.  
o Incase, "No Single Party" is in Clear Majority, President invites “Person” who is likely to command support of Two  
or More Parties which make up Majority in Lok Sabha.  
e Prime Minister is normally Leader of Majority Party in "Lok Sabha".  
  
o However, there have been cases when a "Member of Rajya Sabha" was made Prime Minister.  
  
Tenure of Prime Minister  
  
e Prime Minister holds office for “5 years”.  
  
o But Term of Prime Minister is "Not Fixed" by Indian Constitution.  
  
e Prime Minister holds office "During Pleasure of President".  
  
o However, this does not mean that President can dismiss Prime Minister at Any Time.  
  
e So long as Prime Minister enjoys "Majority Support in Lok Sabha", he cannot be dismissed by President.  
  
o However, if he loses "Confidence of Lok Sabha", he must "Resign" or "President can Dismiss him".  
  
e "Salary and Allowances" of Prime Minister are determined by "Parliament" from time to time.
