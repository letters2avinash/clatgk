PRACTICE QUESTIONS  
  
Which Ministry temporarily blocked Telegram in India?  
(a) Ministry of Home Affairs (b) Ministry of Information and Broadcasting  
(c) Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (d) Ministry of Law and Justice  
  
Which feature of Telegram was allegedly misused by criminal groups to defraud candidates?  
(a) Secret Chat Feature (b) Channel Broadcasting Feature  
(c) Message-Editing Feature (d) Self-Destructing Message Feature  
  
Section 69A of the IT Act 2000 was introduced in which year?  
(a) 2000 (b) 2005 (c) 2008 (d) 2012  
  
Which of the following is NOT listed as a ground for blocking under Section 69A?  
(a) Sovereignty and Integrity of India (b) Public Order  
(c) Prevention of Money Laundering (d) Defence of India  
  
Section 69A empowers which authority to direct intermediaries to block public access to online information?  
(a) Supreme Court of India  
  
(b) Parliament of India  
  
(c) Central Government or an Officer specially authorised by it  
  
(d) Telecom Regulatory Authority of India  
  
The IT (Blocking Rules) of which year govern the procedural mechanism for blocking requests?  
(a) 2000 (b) 2005 (c) 2009 (d) 2011  
  
Which Article of the Indian Constitution protects Digital Free Speech?  
(a) Article 21 (b) Article 14 (c) Article 19(1)(a) (d) Article 19(1)(g)  
  
Which Article allows the State to impose "Reasonable Restrictions" on free speech?  
(a) Article 19(1)(a) (b) Article 21 (c) Article 19(2) (d) Article 14  
  
In Shreya Singhal vs Union of India (2015), what did the Supreme Court do regarding Sections 66A and 69A of the IT  
Act?  
  
(a) Struck down both sections  
  
(b) Upheld both sections  
  
(c) Struck down Section 66A as vague while upholding Section 69A as a narrowly drawn provision  
  
(d) Referred both sections to a Constitutional Bench  
  
What did the Delhi High Court mandate in Tanul Thakur vs Union of India (2022)?  
  
(a) All blocking orders must be approved by Parliament  
  
(b) Foreign companies can challenge blocking orders under Article 19  
  
(c) Disclosure of original blocking orders, challenging blanket confidentiality of Rule 16  
(d) Emergency blocking must be reviewed within 24 hours
