Major Provisions of Delhi EV Policy 2.0  
  
e Mandatory Registration of Electric Vehicles: From 1 January 2027, Only Electric 3 — Wheelers and Electric N1 Category  
Light Commercial Trucks will be Newly Registered.  
  
o. From\! April 2028: Registration of New 2— Wheelers will be restricted exclusively to Electric Models.  
  
o Note: Existing Petrol and Diesel Vehicles already registered in Delhi will be permitted to operate until End of their  
Prescribed Service Life.  
  
e Electrification of School Transport: Schools will be required to convert at least 10% of their Bus Fleet into Electric Buses  
within 2 Years of Policy's Implementation.  
  
o Long-term Objective is to achieve 30% Electrification of School Bus Fleets by 2030.  
  
e Financial Support and Investment: Total Planned Investment amounts to approx \~15000 Crore (Government Allocation:  
27,000 Crore)  
  
o Government support is restricted exclusively to Battery Electric Vehicles, Not to Hybrid Vehicles.  
e Charging Infrastructure Development: Installation of over 30,000 Public Charging Stations across Delhi.  
  
e Implementation Framework  
o Financial Support and Investment: Delhi Transport Department.  
  
o Infrastructure Development: Delhi Transco Limited
